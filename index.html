About CSC — Creative Strategy Cockpit

A portfolio demo built by Seunghoon (Jax) Yeo for the Netflix Coordinator, Creative Strategy — APAC (ex-India) application.

CSC is a working sketch of the day-to-day coordination toolkit this role would use — a single, self-contained page with no back end — built to show how I would bring structure to a lean, newly formed team: tracking a portfolio of work across markets, keeping context moving, and turning priorities into clear next steps.

All names, titles, and numbers are invented sample data — not real Netflix titles, people, or figures.

LivePortfolio tracker, real-time-zone scheduling with a slot finder, market radar, stakeholder directory, and templates — all add / edit / delete and save in your browser.
MockMarket Radar news, the org directory, and message/calendar sending stand in for Netflix internal systems.
